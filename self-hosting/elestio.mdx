[Elestio](https://elest.io/open-source/penpot) is a fully managed platform for self-hosted open-source software. Deploying Penpot through Elestio gives you a dedicated instance on infrastructure you choose — without having to configure DNS, provision SSL certificates, set up SMTP, manage OS updates, or handle Penpot version upgrades yourself. The entire setup takes about three minutes.

<Tip>
  Elestio is the recommended option if you want the data-ownership benefits of self-hosting but don't want to manage the operational details yourself.
</Tip>

## Step 1: Create an Elestio Account

<Note>
  Skip this section if you already have an Elestio account.
</Note>

[Click here to create your Elestio account](https://dash.elest.io/deploy?soft=Penpot&id=121). During sign-up you can choose to deploy on one of five leading cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure, DigitalOcean, Hetzner) or use your own on-premise hardware.

## Step 2: Deploy Penpot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new service">
    In the Elestio dashboard, navigate to **Services** and click **Create Service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Penpot">
    Search for **Penpot** in the software catalogue and select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose cloud provider and region">
    Select the cloud provider and the region closest to your users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a service plan">
    Pick a plan with enough resources for your team. For a team of up to 20 users, the **2 GB RAM** plan is sufficient. Add capacity for larger teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Elestio support level">
    Select the support tier that matches your organization's needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your service">
    Provide a **Service Name** — this appears in the URL of your instance — and an **Admin email** used to create the initial admin account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review advanced configuration (optional)">
    You can set advanced configuration options now or adjust them later from the service dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the service">
    Click **Create Service** in the bottom right. The instance launches in a couple of minutes. When the status shows **Service is running**, you're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Access Your Instance

Once the service is running:

1. Click on the service to open its detail page.
2. Under **Network / CNAME**, find the URL of your instance.
3. Copy and paste the URL into your browser — you'll land on the Penpot login screen.

## Step 4: Configure Penpot on Elestio

To customize your Penpot setup:

1. Click **Update config** in the **Software** section of your service.
2. You'll see the **Docker Compose** file that Elestio uses to run your instance.
3. Switch to the **ENV** tab (top-middle-left) to edit environment variables.
4. Lines starting with `#` are comments and are inactive — delete the `#` to activate a configuration option.
5. After making all your changes, click **Update & restart**. Changes take effect after a couple of minutes.

<Info>
  All configuration variables available for self-hosted Penpot also apply here. See the [Configuration guide](/self-hosting/configuration) for a full reference of flags, auth providers, SMTP, and storage options.
</Info>

For assistance, contact the Penpot team at [support@penpot.app](mailto:support@penpot.app).

## Updates

Elestio updates your Penpot instance automatically when new releases are published. If you prefer to control the upgrade timing:

1. Go to your service's **Software** section.
2. Click **Disable auto updates** under **Software auto updates**.

You can then trigger updates manually at a time that suits your team.
